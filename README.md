# DWS AP Tracker

> **Your event-season checklist**
>
> Keep track of the items you earn during the event season celebration.

## Get started

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. Add an item when you want to track it, then check it off when earned. Your list is saved in that browser on your device.

## At a glance

- Add, mark as earned, or remove items.
- See your earned-item count update as you go.
- No installation or account required.
