📖 Tracker Guide

🎯

Tallying & Steppers

  • [+] / [-] Buttons: Tap to increment or decrement the count by the step value.
  • Step Box (Middle): Tap the number between - and + to change how much you increment per click (e.g. 1, 10, 100).
  • Direct Keyboard Edit: Tap directly on any large blue total count to type in an exact amount.
🖼️

Item Icons

  • Tap Any Icon: Opens the icon selector.
  • Preset Icons: Choose from images stored in your repo's icons/items/ folder.
  • Device Upload: Tap Upload from Device to load screenshot art directly from your gallery.
  • Reset: Restores default artwork if needed.
⚙️

Managing Your List

  • Edit List: Tap Edit List in the header to reveal reorder and delete controls.
  • Reorder (▲ / ▼): Move higher-priority drops to the top of your screen.
  • Delete (✕): Removes an unneeded drop from your tracker.
  • + Add: Type a drop name in the dashed box to create a brand-new tracking item.
💾

Archive, Backup & Sharing

  • 📦 Save & Reset Event: Snapshots all current counts into the Past Events History section with a Month/Year label and resets active counts to 0 for the next event.
  • 📋 Copy Summary: Copies all items with a count greater than 0 to your clipboard, formatted for game chat or Discord.
  • 💾 Backup JSON: Downloads a backup file with your items, counts, icons, and history.
  • 📥 Import Backup: Restores your backup file on any browser or phone without losing data.
