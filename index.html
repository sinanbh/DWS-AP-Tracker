📖 Tracker Guide

🎯

Tallying & Steppers

  • [+] / [-] Buttons: Increment or decrement count by step value.
  • Step Box (Middle): Change step size (e.g. 1, 10, 100).
  • Direct Keyboard Edit: Tap on any count number to type directly.
🖼️

Item Icons

  • Tap Any Icon: Open icon selector to pick presets or upload screenshots.
⚙️

Managing Your List

  • Edit List: Reorder priority (▲ / ▼), remove drops, or add new custom drops.
💾

Archive & Backup

  • Save & Reset Event: Snapshots counts into History and resets current event to 0.
  • Copy Summary: Copies drops with count > 0 to clipboard.
  • Backup / Import: Download JSON or restore across devices.

⚡ Orange Chip Guide

🔨

Crafting Cost

Crafting 1 Orange Chip requires 800 Advanced Chip Materials in the Chip Factory.

📦

Chest Material Yields

  • 🟧 Orange Chip Chest: 100 Materials
  • 🟪 Purple Chip Chest: 30 Materials
  • 🟦 Blue Chip Chest: 5 Materials
  • 🟩 Green Chip Chest: 1 Material
  • 🧪 Advanced Chip Material: 1 Material each
🎁

Direct Choice Chests

Orange Chip Choice Chest grants 1 full Orange Chip directly without requiring any crafting materials.

🧮

Total Calculation Formula

Total Chips = Math.floor(Total Materials / 800) + Choice Chests
